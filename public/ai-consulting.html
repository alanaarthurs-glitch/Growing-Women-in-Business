The bit nobody says

The fear of AI is the fear of asking for help

The fear people have of AI has nothing to do with the technology. It's the same fear as asking anyone for help, wearing a different coat. Most of us are terrible at asking. So we practise on something that never sighs and never judges, and the asking gets easier everywhere else. If you can type a message to a friend, you can do this. I'm sat beside you while you learn.
