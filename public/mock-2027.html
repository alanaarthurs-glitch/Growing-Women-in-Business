is this you?

this is for you if

  • You've poured years into a job, your kids, or a partner, and none of it has your name on it yet.
  • You've thought about starting more than once, looked things up late at night, then talked yourself out of it by morning.
  • You want a push and a room of women, not another course explaining theory you already know.

this isn't for you if

  • You want it done for you, not something you build yourself.
  • You're after a vision board and good vibes, not a room that holds you to the action.
  • You're not willing to do one scary thing this month.

the method underneath: CLIMB

C

Calm the Body

Bring the nervous system down, together. You can't do the brave thing from fight-or-flight.

L

Land It

Say the actual thing, out loud, in public. Not the vague version, the actual number, post, or conversation.

I

In It Together

Never done alone. A fear buddy watches the scary thing happen, and says so.

M

Move

Do it now. The smallest true version, today. Smaller still counts.

B

Bank It

Prove it worked. Log it, in public, proof the other side wasn't as bad as it looked.
