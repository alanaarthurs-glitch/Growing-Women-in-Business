the method underneath: CLIMB

Five steps. Every one works on four things at once: your mindset, your voice, your business (whether you're starting it or scaling it) and the woman you're growing into.

C

Centre the Body

First, a body reset. Some days that means calming a wired nervous system down, other days it means shaking you up and getting the energy going. Then the business goes on the table as plain facts: what comes in, what goes out, what you're actually selling, and what your week is really carrying.

L

Land It

Say the actual thing, out loud. What the business is for in your life, the number that would change things, and one sentence with a price on it. Saying it until it stops wobbling is how you get used to your own voice.

I

In It Together

Never done alone. We find out whose voice is talking you out of it, then build the business side with AI, side by side: your first page and price if you're starting, the systems and back office if you're ready to grow.

M

Move

Do it now, the smallest true version, today. The price goes public, the post goes up, or the ask goes to a real person. Something exists in the world with your name and your voice on it.

B

Bank It

Prove it worked, then make it stick. What fear predicted against what actually happened. Then the woman you're growing into: one who eats well, drinks enough water, sleeps properly, keeps an honest eye on the wine, the vape or anything else she leans on, and never stops learning. Plus the numbers to watch and your next three steps.
