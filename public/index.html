The method

The day runs on CLIMB

Five steps, one day: the mind-body reset, goal setting and life planning, money and financial planning, the business chat, AI wired into your business, and a Q and A before you leave. Each step works on you first, then on the business, because the business grows out of you and not the other way round.

C L I M B
C

Calm the Body

The mind-body reset. Nothing gets decided from fight-or-flight. A walk or a breath, then an honest look at what you're carrying.

Then the business gets the same: the numbers, and what you're actually selling today. Facts, no story.

L

Land It

Goal setting and life planning. What the business is for, and the number that would change things. Then the fear script in its exact words, and whose voice it is.

Then one sentence: I help ___ with ___ for £___. Your price, said out loud and tested against what you actually need to live on.

I

In It Together

The business chat. You're not doing this into thin air. The years you gave everyone else's thing, turned into named skills, and an honest talk about what's in the way.

Then AI wired into your business, on a screen you can see. Website, offer, posts, back office. You type. You keep every prompt.

M

Move

The scary thing, done in the room, today, with me there. Not next month.

The page goes live and the price goes public. Something with your name on it exists before you leave.

B

Bank It

What fear said against what actually happened, written down. Your own ten-minute daily practice, and a Q and A for everything you've been afraid to ask.

Then the money plan: what the business must earn by when, and the numbers to watch. Thirty days of check-ins with me, and The Circle as the floor under you.

Mentoring, not therapy. If something comes up on the day that belongs with a specialist, I'll say so, and help you find them.
