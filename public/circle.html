How it works

Not another feed to scroll past. A rhythm that actually holds you.

This isn't a group chat that goes quiet after week two. It's a weekly shape: name the brave thing, do the brave thing, get seen doing the brave thing, then do it again next week, with someone right beside you the whole time.

Monday

The Monday Commitment

One small brave action for the week, in one sentence, posted to the room: "This week I will ___, and the scary bit is ___."

Tuesday to Friday

The 10-Minute Check-In

Two lines a day. One on your action, one on the baseline habit you're tracking. Ten minutes, that's the cap.

Midweek

The Live

A real weekly call, about an hour, run live. Hot-seat coaching, then a round where anyone who's stalled restates a smaller version.

Friday

The Wins Thread

Your win, logged and seen by the room, confirmed by your fear buddy. Small counts. Small is the point.

Monthly

Re-pair and Reset

Fresh fear buddy if you want one, and you pick the one baseline habit you'll log for the month.

The method

The Circle runs on ORBIT

An orbit doesn't climb to a peak. It goes round again, every week, on purpose.

O R B I T
O

Open Up

Before anything else, come down out of the day and into the room. A breath, a downshift, a quick baseline check.

R

Reveal It

Name the one small brave action for the week, out loud, publicly, in the Monday thread.

B

Buddy Up

Never done alone. Your fear buddy replies first, watches it happen, confirms the win.

I

Inch Forward

If it's stalled, shrink it. A smaller version still counts, always.

T

Track It

Post the win in the Friday thread, buddy-confirmed, proof that the other side wasn't as bad as it looked.
