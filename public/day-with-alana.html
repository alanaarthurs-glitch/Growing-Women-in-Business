How the day runs

Five steps, one day

The day runs on CLIMB. Every step works on four things at once: your mindset, your voice, your business and the woman you're growing into.

C

Centre the Body

A body reset first, then the business goes on the table as plain facts: what comes in, what goes out, what you're actually selling, and what your week is really carrying.

L

Land It

Say the actual thing, out loud. What the business is for in your life, the number that would change things, and one sentence with a price on it.

I

In It Together

We find out whose voice is talking you out of it, then build the business side with AI, side by side: your first page and price if you're starting, the systems and back office if you're ready to grow.

M

Move

Do it now, the smallest true version, today. The price goes public, the post goes up, or the ask goes to a real person.

B

Bank It

Prove it worked, then make it stick. What fear predicted against what actually happened, the numbers to watch and your next three steps.
