Privacy

Your details, in plain English

This is what Growing Women in Business collects, why, where it goes and how to have it deleted. Growing Women in Business is run by Alana Arthurs. Any question about your details, email hello@alanaarthurs.com.

What we collect

The findable scorecard

Your first name and email address. Your score, your score in each of the three areas and the two answers about timing and what's in the way. Whether you ticked the box for the newsletter. If you arrived from a link with tracking tags on it, the name of the place you came from.

We use it to show your result, email you a copy, and know who might want a call. You only join the newsletter if you tick the box or ask to be added.

The Future Maker quiz

Your email address and the type you got. We email you the guide for your type and add you to the newsletter.

The newsletter

Your email address and which page you signed up on. We use it to send The Only Way Is Up, every other Sunday.

The application and the welcome page

Your name and email address, your phone number if you give it, and what you write in the form. We use it to reply to you and decide together whether working together is right for you.

Payments

Payments go through PayPal. Your card details go to PayPal, not to us. PayPal tells us that a payment was made.

Where it's kept

Form answers are saved in Notion, which is where Alana keeps her list of contacts. The server that runs this site also keeps a short backup copy. Emails (your scorecard result, your quiz guide) are sent through a service called Resend. These companies hold the data on our behalf.

We keep your details while you're on the newsletter or talking to us. Ask and we'll delete them.

Cookies

This site doesn't use analytics or advertising cookies, and doesn't set any cookies of its own. Fonts load from Google Fonts, so Google sees that your browser asked for them. The payment pages load PayPal's buttons, and PayPal may set its own cookies when you pay.

Unsubscribe, see or delete your details

To leave the newsletter, reply to any email and say so, or email hello@alanaarthurs.com. To see what we hold about you, correct it or have it deleted, email the same address. We'll sort it and confirm.

If you're not happy with how we've handled your details, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
