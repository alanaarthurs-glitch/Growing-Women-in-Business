Who this is for

Is this you?

The method

Not another course. Not a vision board.

This isn't information you already know and aren't using. It's a room, a method, and someone who won't let you disappear into the back of it. The practical business step sits on top of the part nobody else touches: your nervous system, your routines, the small daily stuff that's already deciding whether you follow through, long before any spreadsheet tells you the truth.

C L I M B
C

Calm the Body

Bring the nervous system down, together. You can't do the brave thing from fight-or-flight.

The same 10-minute opening block, every single week, plus the Week 3 in-the-moment tools.

L

Land It

Say the actual thing, out loud, in public. Not the vague version, the actual number, post, or conversation.

Week 1 defines exactly what "done" means. Week 2 you say the script out loud in the room.

I

In It Together

Never done alone. A fear buddy watches the scary thing happen, and says so.

Buddy beats every week from 2 to 5: rehearsal, on-call, pact-signing, witnessing.

M

Move

Do it now. The smallest true version, today. Smaller still counts.

Week 1 slices it into five steps. Week 3 takes the small real-world version out there.

B

Bank It

Prove it worked. Log it, in public, proof the other side wasn't as bad as it looked.

The tally board throughout, and Week 4's evidence review.

The six weeks

From the moment you pay

Nothing here waits for a Monday.

  1. The moment you pay: one prompt. In one sentence, tell Alana the one big action you're here to take.
  2. Within 48 hours: a personal call from Alana. She welcomes you in, reflects your action back, sorts your fear buddy, and confirms your kickoff date.
  3. Kickoff, live with your whole cohort: everyone takes the first tiny version of their action in the room. Nobody leaves having only talked.
  4. Week 1, Name It and Shrink It.
  5. Week 2, Say It in the Room.
  6. Week 3, Do It Small, Out There.
  7. Week 4, Stage the Real Thing.
  8. Week 5, Action Week.
  9. Week 6, Lock It In. You graduate a Future Maker, and The Circle is there to land in afterwards.
